    Behind every great product there are three steps.

    Step 01

    Discover

    We start with a conversation. I dig into your product, your users and your goals, so we're clear on what to build and why it matters.

    • Discovery call
    • User research
    • Product audit
    • Scope & timeline

    Step 02

    Design

    Flows, interfaces and a design system come together. You see progress in Figma every week, not after a month of silence.

    • Wireframes
    • UI design
    • Design system
    • Prototyping
    • Usability testing

    Step 03

    Build & Ship

    I turn the design into production-ready front-end with motion and polish, and stay on after launch so everything works as promised.

    • React & TypeScript
    • Motion & interaction
    • QA & accessibility
    • Launch
    • Post-launch support
